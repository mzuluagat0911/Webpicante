Questions

What Pulse is and what you do not have to guess.

What is Pulse?

Pulse is web software from Picante Studio for businesses with a physical location. It monitors the area on Google Maps, compares the competition, and replies to reviews in the brand's voice.

Who is Pulse for?

It is for restaurant owners, retail and local services, and operations teams that run several locations in cities across the US and Latin America.

What does Pulse watch on Google Maps?

It watches the competitor ranking in your radius, their rating, the volume and content of reviews, and changes in profile activity. That shows who is ahead in the area and what moved.

How is Pulse different from checking Google Maps by hand?

Checking the map by hand does not compare every location against every competitor, and it does not alert you when one overtakes you. Pulse does that follow-up every week and can reply to reviews in your brand's voice, without a generic template.
